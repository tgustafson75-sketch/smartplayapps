How The Sparks Report works

A free directory of programs for veterans, service members and military families, with a tool that turns your picks into emails and a plan. It takes about 10 minutes.

1. Find programs

  1. Go to Find help.
  2. Type what you need in everyday words: housing, job, emergency money, PTSD, service dog, scholarship, start a business, golf, fishing. Common words are matched automatically, so “dog” also finds “canine” and “K9”.
  3. Pick your state. Nationwide programs stay in the list too. To see only local ones, untick “Include nationwide programs”.
  4. Own a business or starting one? Tap Help for my business above the search box, then pick your stage, needs and owner type on the left. The list narrows to programs that fit, and your packet emails are written from your business.
  5. Use the left-hand filters. What you need is the type of help. Who it's for covers groups like women veterans, spouses, caregivers, Purple Heart recipients or a specific branch. Tap a filter again to turn it off, or tap the ✕ on the orange tags above the results.

2. Read the colored label on each program

Every website was opened by an automated check, which runs again every week.

Site verifiedThe website opened normally.
Moved — new addressThe program moved. The button already uses the new address.
Now redirects to another orgThe old address opens a different organization. It may have merged or closed, so confirm before applying.
Couldn't confirm pageThe site responded but blocked our check. It usually works in a normal browser.
Website goneThe site no longer exists. Hidden unless you untick “Hide programs whose website is gone”.
Unsafe — link removedThe old address now belongs to an unrelated site such as a casino. Don't visit it.

3. Build your packet

  1. Tap + Packet on each program you want to contact. The counter on My packet shows how many you've added.
  2. Open My packet and fill in About you. Name, location and “What I need help with” matter most. One or two specific sentences work best, for example: “I'm transitioning out in March and need help finding stable housing for my family of four.”
  3. Outreach emails gives one email per program. Tap Copy, then paste it into your email or into the program's contact form. You can also read it as a phone script.
  4. Packet outline is your one-page plan: your situation, the programs you picked, a document checklist and an outreach log.
  5. Action steps gives a short to-do list for each program, from opening the site to following up.

4. Follow through

  • Gather your documents first. Most programs ask for your DD-214 and a photo ID.
  • Write down the date you contact each program in the outreach log.
  • No reply in 7 days? Follow up once by phone or email.
  • Stuck, or a program has closed? Call 2-1-1 for local referrals, or ask your county Veterans Service Officer (VSO). VSOs help with VA claims at no cost.

Questions

Is my information saved or shared?
What you type in “About you” and the programs in your packet stay in your own browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data erases them.
Do the emails send themselves?
No. You copy them and send them yourself, so you can edit them first.
Where does the list come from?
It started from a veteran resource spreadsheet last updated in 2017. Every link was re-checked in November 2026 and current national programs were added. See Updates for what changes each week.
Is this an official VA site?
No. It's an independent directory. Always confirm eligibility and details directly with each program.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. On a phone the filters appear above the results.
