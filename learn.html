The ladder
  • Foundations. A working app at a link. Context, GitHub, going live, spend caps.
  • Builder. An assistant that knows your files, mail and calendar.
  • Operator. One command center for your business: traffic, tasks, issues.
  • Advanced. Apps that are guarded, gated and cost-controlled.
How we teach
  • One question at a time, with two clear choices. Never a blank page.
  • Short answers. Plain words first; the jargon gets named after you’ve built the thing.
  • Whole, then parts. Hold the working app first. Break it down after.
  • Think in systems. Loops, links and dependencies, drawn on one page.
