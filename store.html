App store submission

Getting into the App Store is hard. We’ve done it.

We shipped SmartPlay Caddie to the App Store and Google Play, rejections included. Now we do it for builders, through approval.

  1. Developer account setup, including the organization account
  2. App Store Connect record, subscriptions and pricing
  3. Listing: name, keywords, screenshots, privacy and terms pages
  4. Privacy and age-rating questions
  5. Build signing and submission
  6. Rejection fixes and App Review replies
  7. The same for Google Play

Apple requires the final submit to come from your own developer account. We prepare everything and walk you through that step.

Questions

Store questions

Is the SmartPlay store open?

Not yet. This page is a preview. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you when builders can list apps.

Who owns an app I sell on the store?

You do. It’s your app, your users and your listing. We take a percentage of each sale, and builders on higher Launch Pack tiers keep more of each sale.

Can you put my app on the App Store or Google Play?

We prepare everything: developer account, listing, screenshots, privacy pages, review questions and rejection replies. Apple requires the final submit to come from your own developer account, so we walk you through that step.

Is every app on the store reviewed?

Yes. Nothing is listed until it passes our security review, because every app on the shelf carries the SmartPlay name.
