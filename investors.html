The problem02

Golfers get numbers. What they need is the decision.

On the course

Golf apps give yardages and charts. The golfer still has to work out the club, the target and the miss to favor while standing over the ball.

In practice

Real coaching is scarce and costs money, so most practice happens with no feedback at all, and the same faults get repeated.

In the simulator

Simulator golf is growing fast. A bay produces data on every shot, but turning that data into a lesson still needs a person in the room.

Most AI products answer with confidence even when they are guessing. In a sport where trust decides whether advice gets followed, that is a product defect.

Smart Intelligence Engine06

Five reusable capabilities, each already running in Caddie

01

User Model

Player profiles and roster, swing and round history, coach notes with attribution, and tendencies tracked across sessions.

02

Context Engine

A GPS pipeline that rejects outlier fixes, uses authoritative course geometry and satellite imagery, and always respects a manual override.

03

Decision Engine

A tiered reasoning cascade. A local pre-classifier runs first, then a fast router, then a heavier model for substantive judgment.

04

Learning Engine

Swing capture that builds a labeled dataset, tendencies mapped to drills, and outcomes captured as part of the product rather than as telemetry.

05

Interaction Engine

Press-to-talk voice, camera swing analysis and acoustic strike detection.

Any new product gets these on day one, so the cost of building it is mostly the experience layer on top.

Venues12

From the indoor muni to the course

An indoor muni is an affordable, walk-in simulator venue: the public course of indoor golf. It is where new and returning golfers start, and SmartBay AI runs the lessons there around the clock, with no staff needed in the bay.

Step 1

Indoor muni

A golfer walks in, scans a QR code and gets a full AI lesson in the bay: baseline swings, one fix, a drill and a retest.

Step 2

Practice and play

The same player keeps the same caddie at home in SmartPlay Golf and on the range, so every session adds to one record.

Step 3

The course

SmartPlay Caddie goes with them onto a real course, calling the club, the target and the miss from what the bay already learned.

SmashLab, an independent venue brand, licenses SmartBay AI and Caddie.
