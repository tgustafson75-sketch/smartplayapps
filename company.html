Smart Intelligence Engine

The platform claim, and what actually runs

Five reusable capabilities sit under the portfolio. Not a diagram drawn after the fact — each one is an abstraction of a component already shipping in SmartPlay Caddie.

CapabilityRunning today in SmartPlay Caddie
01

User Model

Player profiles and roster, per-swing history, round and score history, coach notes with attribution, and tendencies derived across sessions.

02

Context Engine

A GPS pipeline with outlier rejection, authoritative course geometry, satellite imagery — and a manual override the system respects without argument.

03

Decision Engine

A tiered reasoning cascade: a heavier model for substantive judgment, a fast one for routing, and a local pre-classifier ahead of both to cut latency and spend.

04

Learning Engine

Swing capture building a labeled dataset, tendencies mapped to drills, shot and score logging, session review — outcome capture as a product surface, not backend telemetry.

05

Interaction Engine

Press-to-talk voice with a locked brand narrator, camera swing analysis with skeletal overlay, and acoustic strike detection in a practice cage.

Founder

Built by an operator, not a lab

Veteran Owned and Developed

SmartPlay AI was founded by Tim Gustafson — ten years in uniform, four in the Marine Corps as a military policeman and six in the Army as a medic and radiology technologist, followed by 26 years in healthcare operations, including COO leadership in radiology and imaging.

That background is the company's thesis in miniature. The products are aimed at the moment a decision gets made under pressure, with incomplete information, by someone who will own the outcome — because that describes most of the founder's working life.

He doesn't build alone: fellow veterans test every build, advisors pressure-test the business, and ambassadors bring SmartPlay into their own communities.
