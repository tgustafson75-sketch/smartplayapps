What to do first

Your tasks, sorted by what matters

Every task lands in one of four boxes: do now, schedule, hand off, or drop. You decide what’s important. SmartManage works out what’s urgent from the due date, so the list in front of you is always in the right order.

Inside the app

One place for the whole day

Ask Roger by typing or talking. He knows your tasks, your calendar and the people you work with, so his answers are about your day, not a generic one.
